# Whoami?

## Who are you?
An multifunction fun bot that tells jokes, mention phrases, react to certain texts and most importantly, says who are **YOU**.

Commands:
    
    /whoami - says who are you
    /whoishelp - tells you commands
    /whysoserious - tells a joke.
    /phrases - tells you a phrase from an api.
